# NextRole — SaaS Landing-Page Copywriting Case Study

NextRole is a fictional AI-assisted job-search platform created to demonstrate SaaS copywriting, messaging strategy, UX content, responsive interface design and Nuxt development.

The project transforms generic, feature-focused AI messaging into a clearer customer experience built around organization, confidence and control.

> **Portfolio notice:** NextRole is a fictional concept project. It does not represent a paid client engagement or a functioning commercial service. It is not affiliated with any existing company or product using a similar name.

## Project Overview

An active job search can quickly become difficult to manage. Opportunities are saved across job boards, application deadlines live in spreadsheets, documents require repeated editing and important follow-ups can easily be forgotten.

NextRole presents a unified workspace where job seekers can:

- Organize employment opportunities
- Track application progress
- Prepare role-specific application materials
- Practice for interviews
- Schedule follow-ups
- Maintain control over AI-generated suggestions

## The Copywriting Challenge

The original concept relied on broad statements such as:

> “The Best AI Job-Search Platform”

Although this described the product category, it did not clearly communicate:

- Who the product was designed for
- What customer problem it solved
- How it differed from other AI tools
- Why a visitor should trust it
- What action the visitor should take

## Messaging Strategy

### Primary audience

Mid-career professionals actively managing multiple job applications, interviews and follow-ups.

### Customer problem

The job-search process is fragmented across browser tabs, job boards, spreadsheets, emails, documents and personal notes.

### Customer desire

A more organized and manageable job search—not simply another automatic AI writing tool.

### Core value proposition

NextRole brings job tracking, application tailoring, interview preparation and follow-up planning into one intelligent workspace.

### Primary conversion goal

Encourage visitors to create a free account.

## Before and After

### Before

> “The Best AI Job-Search Platform”

The original headline was generic, product-centred and based on an unsupported superlative.

### After

> “Run your job search. Don’t let it run you.”

The revised headline acknowledges the customer’s frustration and positions the product as a way to regain structure and control.

## Key Copywriting Decisions

### Customer problem before product features

The landing page begins by recognizing scattered applications, repetitive document editing and missed follow-ups.

### Benefits instead of feature labels

Features are connected to practical outcomes. For example, application tracking becomes:

> “Know exactly where every opportunity stands and what you need to do next.”

### Responsible AI positioning

The copy explains that AI supports research, organization and drafting while the user reviews every document and controls every submission.

### Lower-risk call to action

“Start your job search free” gives visitors a clearer action and reduces perceived commitment.

### Objection handling

The FAQ addresses automatic applications, invented experience, managing several opportunities and using an existing resume.
